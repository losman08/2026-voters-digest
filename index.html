How to use this guide

A quick tour of each part — close with the ✕ when you're done.

📍

Choose your state

Tap the flag and state name at the top-left. You'll see which states are covered, switch between them, or request one that isn't listed yet.

⏳

The countdown

The colored bar shows how many days remain until Election Day, November 3. It turns amber in the final week and red on Election Day.

📋

Measures

Each card explains one ballot measure in plain English: what it does, what Yes and No mean, the cost, who backs and opposes it, and official links — including the full legal text as written by lawmakers.

🎯

Show the impact for you

On the Measures tab, pick one or more situations — renter, homeowner, senior, and so on. Each measure then shows how it could affect people like you. Use the topic chips or search to narrow the list.

👤

Candidates

Candidates shown side by side: background, where they stand issue by issue, endorsements, and a “Record & controversies” section that labels each item by how well it's verified. No scandal is invented.

🗳️

Official links to find your polling place and ballot drop boxes, track your mail ballot, and register — all from the Secretary of State.

A note before you begin

Dear Family and Friends,

Every election year cycle, I hear many of the frustrations from friends and family: “I don’t even know what I’m voting on, and nothing explains it clearly.” The ballot’s full of legalese, the official guides send you down ten different rabbit holes, and everyone’s shouting an opinion. Not to mention the media blitz with campaign flyers flooding your mailboxes, plastered all over TV ads, etc. So I decided to pull this digest together to help summarize what your votes are counted towards.

It’s non-partisan and plain-English, all in one place: what each measure and candidate actually does, what a Yes or No really means, who’s for and against, and how it might touch your life — with links back to the official sources whenever you want to dig in. The intention is to never sway you on how, or who, to vote for. That call is yours, always. This is just to serve as a voter-friendly format to pull all of it together in a single place and help make your decision easier to make on which choice matters most to you.

Voter’s Digest is here for educational and informational purposes only — a head start, not the final word. Pick your state up top, take your time, and vote with confidence.

Respectfully,
Carlos
